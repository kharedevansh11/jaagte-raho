# Cold-call script: fleet owners

**Goal of the call:** not to sell. Get a yes to a **free 30-day pilot with 2–3 of their vehicles**.
The strongest move is a **live demo during the call**: you trigger a check-in to the owner's own phone, so they hear it themselves.

**Before you dial:** have the dashboard open, and the "try it now" page ready to ring them.
**Rules:** smile while talking, keep each part short, ask and then *listen*. Never argue with an objection; agree, then reframe.

---

## Version 1: Hinglish (Hindi words, written in English)

**1. Opener (10 seconds)**
> Namaste, kya main [Owner name] ji se baat kar raha hoon?
> Ji, main [Your name] bol raha hoon, Jaagte Raho se. Bas do minute chahiye aapke, abhi baat karna theek rahega?

*(If busy: "Bilkul ji. Kab call karoon, shaam ko ya kal subah?" Note the time and hang up politely.)*

**2. Reason for calling**
> Main aapko isliye call kar raha hoon kyunki aapki gaadiyaan raat mein bhi chalti hain.
> Ek seedha sawaal: raat ko 2–3 baje jab aapka driver highway pe hota hai, aapko kaise pata chalta hai ki woh jaag raha hai ya use neend aa rahi hai?

*(Pause. Let them answer. Usually: "Pata nahi chalta" or "Main kabhi kabhi call kar leta hoon.")*

**3. Find the pain**
> Kabhi aisa hua hai ki kisi driver ko neend aa gayi ho, ya chhota-mota accident hua ho raat mein?
> Aur aisa kuch ho jaaye toh aapka kitna nuksaan hota hai: gaadi, maal, insurance, aur sabse badi baat, driver ki jaan?

*(Listen. Repeat their words back: "Matlab ek accident se kaafi bada nuksaan ho jaata hai.")*

**4. The pitch (30 seconds)**
> Isi ke liye humne Jaagte Raho banaya hai.
> Raat ki shift mein hamari AI didi har 20–30 minute mein aapke driver ko call karti hai. Unki apni bhasha mein, bilkul pyaar se: "Kahan pahunche? Khana khaya?"
> Baat karte karte woh sunti hai ki driver ki awaaz mein neend toh nahi hai.
> Agar driver bahut neend mein lage, ya do baar call na uthaye, toh turant aapko phone aata hai, driver ka naam aur last location ke saath.
> Koi app nahi, koi camera nahi, koi machine nahi lagani. Driver ke paas koi bhi phone ho, chalega.

**5. Live demo (the closer)**
> Sunne se zyada achha hai aap khud sun lijiye. Main abhi aapke number pe ek demo call karwa deta hoon, ek minute ki. Aap driver ban ke baat kariye, chahe neend wali awaaz mein bhi. Theek hai?

*(Trigger the call. Stay on the line or call back right after.)*
> Kaisa laga? … Aur yeh aapke dashboard pe aisa dikhta hai: kaun jaag raha hai, kise call karna hai, sab ek jagah.

**6. Close for the pilot**
> Toh mera suggestion yeh hai: aap apni 2–3 gaadiyaan chuniye jo raat mein chalti hain. 30 din bilkul free chalaate hain.
> Aapko sirf driver ka naam aur number dena hai, baaki setup hum kar denge.
> Is hafte shuru karein, ya agle somvaar se?

*(Give two options, never yes or no. Once they pick, confirm on WhatsApp right away.)*

**7. Wrap-up**
> Bahut shukriya [Owner name] ji. Main abhi WhatsApp pe details bhej raha hoon. Aapke drivers ka hum poora dhyan rakhenge. Jaagte raho!

## Version 2: English

**1. Opener (10 seconds)**
> Hi, am I speaking with [Owner name]?
> This is [Your name] from Jaagte Raho. I just need two minutes. Is now a good time?

*(If busy: "Of course. Would this evening or tomorrow morning be better?" Note the time and end politely.)*

**2. Reason for calling**
> I'm calling because your vehicles run through the night.
> A quick question: at 2 or 3 am, when your driver is out on the highway, how do you know whether he's alert or starting to doze off?

*(Pause and let them answer.)*

**3. Find the pain**
> Has a driver ever fallen asleep at the wheel, or had a close call at night?
> And when something like that happens, what does it cost you: the vehicle, the cargo, the insurance, and most of all, the driver's life?

*(Listen. Reflect it back: "So one incident can cost far more than anything else on the balance sheet.")*

**4. The pitch (30 seconds)**
> That's exactly why we built Jaagte Raho.
> During the night shift, our AI calls your driver every 20 to 30 minutes, in his own language, like a friend checking in: "Where have you reached? Have you eaten?"
> While they talk, it listens for signs of sleepiness.
> If the driver sounds very drowsy, or misses two calls, you get a phone call immediately, with his name and last known location.
> No app, no camera, no hardware. It works on any phone.

**5. Live demo**
> Rather than describe it, let me show you. I'll trigger a one-minute demo call to your number right now. Answer it as if you were the driver, even sleepily if you like. Okay?

*(Trigger the call.)*
> How did that feel? And this is what you'd see on your dashboard: who's awake and who needs a call, all in one place.

**6. Close for the pilot**
> Here's what I'd suggest: pick two or three vehicles that run at night, and we'll run it free for 30 days.
> All we need is each driver's name and number; we handle the setup.
> Should we start this week, or next Monday?

**7. Wrap-up**
> Thank you, [Owner name]. I'll send the details on WhatsApp right now. We'll look after your drivers. Jaagte raho!

## Objection handling

| They say | You say (Hinglish) | You say (English) |
|---|---|---|
| "Kitna paisa lagega?" / "What does it cost?" | "Pehle 30 din bilkul free hain. Uske baad har gaadi ka ek chhota sa monthly charge, ek accident ke nuksaan ke saamne kuch bhi nahi. Pehle dekh lijiye kaam karta hai ya nahi." | "The first 30 days are completely free. After that it's a small monthly fee per vehicle, a fraction of what a single accident costs. See if it works for you first." |
| "Driver pareshan honge baar baar call se" / "Drivers will find it annoying" | "Call 45 second se kam ki hoti hai, pyaar se, unki bhasha mein. Sawaari ho toh 10 second mein khatam. Aur raat ke 3 baje akele driver ko kisi se baat karna achha hi lagta hai." | "Each call is under 45 seconds, friendly and in their language, and it ends in 10 seconds if there's a passenger. Honestly, at 3 am a lone driver usually welcomes someone to talk to." |
| "Humare paas GPS hai" / "We already have GPS" | "GPS batata hai gaadi kahan hai. Jaagte Raho batata hai driver jaag raha hai ya nahi. Dono saath mein kaam karte hain." | "GPS tells you where the truck is. Jaagte Raho tells you whether the driver is awake. They work together." |
| "Main khud call kar leta hoon" / "I call them myself" | "Bahut achhi baat hai. Par 3 baje aapko bhi neend chahiye. Hum har driver ko har 20 minute call karte hain, aur aapko sirf tab jagaate hain jab sach mein zaroorat ho." | "That's great. But you need sleep too. We call every driver every 20 minutes, and only wake you when it really matters." |
| "Soch ke batata hoon" / "Let me think about it" | "Bilkul ji. Bas ek kaam kijiye: ek driver se shuru karte hain, free mein, ek hafte ke liye. Pasand na aaye toh band kar dena." | "Of course. How about this: let's start with just one driver, free, for a week. If you don't like it, we switch it off." |
| "AI pe bharosa nahi" / "I don't trust AI" | "Isiliye pehle demo. Aap khud sun lijiye, phir decide kijiye. Aur last decision hamesha aapka: hum sirf aapko batate hain, phone aap karte hain." | "That's why we start with a demo. Hear it yourself, then decide. And you're always in control: we only alert you; you make the call." |

## If they don't pick up (WhatsApp, after one missed call)

**Hinglish**
> Namaste [Owner name] ji, main [Your name], Jaagte Raho se. Raat mein aapke drivers ko neend aaye toh hum turant aapko bata dete hain: AI har 20 minute driver se baat karti hai, koi app ya camera nahi. 2–3 gaadiyon pe 30 din free pilot chalaana chahenge? Ek minute ka demo call bhi karwa sakta hoon.

**English**
> Hi [Owner name], this is [Your name] from Jaagte Raho. We alert fleet owners the moment a night-shift driver sounds sleepy: an AI checks in with each driver every 20 minutes, with no app or camera. Would you like a free 30-day pilot on 2–3 vehicles? Happy to send you a one-minute demo call too.
